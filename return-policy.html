Return & Refund Policy

MKG GLOBAL · Last updated: October 2026
7-Day Easy Returns
Eligible products may be returned within 7 days of delivery, subject to the conditions below. Returns are handled through courier.

1. Return Eligibility

MKG GLOBAL accepts returns for eligible defective and non-defective products within 7 days of delivery.

  • The product must be returned in the condition required for the return to be accepted.
  • Included accessories, documents, packaging and other supplied items should be returned where applicable.
  • For device-related concerns, MKG GLOBAL may inspect the product before approving a return, exchange or refund.

2. How to Request a Return

Contact MKG GLOBAL within the 7-day return window with your order or invoice details and reason for return. Our team will provide return instructions and courier details after reviewing the request.

3. Return Method & Courier Charges

Returns are handled by courier. Return courier charges are handled case-by-case depending on the reason and circumstances of the return. The applicable arrangement will be communicated before the return shipment is arranged.

4. Inspection

After receipt, MKG GLOBAL will inspect the returned product and verify the reported issue, product condition and return eligibility. The final return, refund or exchange outcome is subject to this inspection.

5. Refund Processing

Once the returned product is received and inspection is completed, an approved refund will be processed within 5 working days.

6. Exchanges

MKG GLOBAL accepts exchanges for eligible products. Exchange requests are subject to product availability and verification of the returned product.

7. Important Note

Return and refund eligibility may vary according to the product, its condition and the reason for return. Please contact MKG GLOBAL before sending any product back. Do not ship a return without receiving return instructions from our team.
